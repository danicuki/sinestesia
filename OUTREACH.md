# Sinestesia — Agency Outreach Playbook

A practical, week-by-week plan to get Sinestesia booked through event agencies,
wedding planners and conference organizers. Companion to `MONETIZATION.md`
(Phase 1) and the landing page in `site/`.

## The one thing to understand about agencies

Agencies don't buy shows — they **resell solutions to their clients' brief**. The
brief they get constantly is: *"give us something innovative / techy / memorable
that photographs well."* Sinestesia is a ready-made answer to that brief. So the
pitch is never "we're a band with visuals"; it's:

> **"When your client asks for something with AI that isn't a gimmick, this is the
> act you pull out. We make you look ahead of the market."**

Everything below serves that framing.

## Prerequisites (before the first email goes out)

1. **The 60–90s showreel.** Cut from the NFC Summit footage: open on the wall
   painting itself (5s), one shot of the audience reacting, one close-up of a lyric
   appearing as a drawing, end card with the wordmark and URL. Vertical (9:16) and
   horizontal (16:9) exports. This does 80% of the selling.
2. **The landing pages live** on a real domain (e.g. `sinestesia.art`), booking
   email on that domain. There are two offers on two pages — keep them separate
   in outreach too: `index.html` sells the full act (agencies, planners),
   `artists.html` sells "we paint your show" (musicians, bands, managers).
3. **A one-page PDF** ("agency sheet"): photo, 3-line description, formats &
   durations, net rates, what the venue must provide (condensed rider), contacts.
   Agencies forward PDFs internally; make it forwardable.
4. **Portuguese versions** of the reel captions, page and PDF — the Lisbon/Porto
   market sells in PT.

## Who to target (in order of conversion likelihood)

| Tier | Segment | Why | Where to find them |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Event production agencies & DMCs (Lisbon, Porto, Algarve) | They program corporate dinners, incentives and launches weekly; Portugal is a huge MICE destination | Google Maps ("event agency Lisboa", "DMC Portugal"), Event Point directory, LinkedIn |
| A | Brand-experience / activation agencies | "AI experience" is literally in their client briefs right now | LinkedIn, agency award shortlists (Eventex, Prémios Eventos) |
| B | High-end wedding planners | One yes = recurring referrals; the first-dance angle is unique inventory for them | Zankyou, instagram wedding-planner hashtags PT, venue preferred-supplier lists |
| B | Conference/tech-event organizers | The show doubles as a keynote-grade demo; budgets sit in "speakers & entertainment" | Web Summit orbit, AI/dev meetup organizers, corp. innovation teams |
| C | Festival programmers & cultural venues | Slower cycles, lower fees, but great footage and legitimacy | Festival open calls, municipal cultural programming |

Target: a list of **50 Tier-A/B contacts** with a named person each (producer,
creative director, or founder — not info@).

## The offer that makes agencies say yes

- **Net rates + suggested retail.** Give agencies a net price list and let them
  mark up 15–25% (or quote retail and pay 15% commission — let *them* choose the
  model; some prefer markup, some commission).
- **White-label option.** They can present the act inside their own proposal deck;
  send them 3 slides they can paste in (photos + description + tech needs).
- **Fast quotes.** Promise (and deliver) a quote within 24h — agencies lose
  pitches waiting on suppliers.
- **A "safe" story.** One line that kills their risk objection: *performed live
  at NFC Summit Lisbon — here's the footage — and the venue needs are a one-page
  rider: projector, PA and a stable internet connection.*

## Outreach sequence

**Rule: the video link goes in every first message. Nobody books text.**

- **Day 0 — Email #1** (short, PT or EN as appropriate):

  > Subject: An act that paints your client's event, live
  >
  > Hi {name} — we're a Lisbon duo with something your clients haven't seen: while
  > the singer performs, an AI paints one continuous artwork on the stage wall,
  > built from the lyrics in real time. 90 seconds of it live: {reel link}.
  >
  > It premiered at NFC Summit Lisbon. We travel with the creative rig; the venue
  > provides standard AV (projector, PA, internet). 15–40 min formats, net rates
  > for agencies.
  >
  > Worth 15 minutes? I'll do a live 2-minute mini-demo on the call — I sing, you
  > watch the painting appear.

- **Day 3 — LinkedIn**: connect with the same person, one line + the reel.
- **Day 10 — Email #2**: different asset (the keepsake angle — photo of a final
  canvas print: "the client keeps the painting"). One paragraph.
- **Day 30+ — quarterly touch**: every new performance produces new footage; send
  the best 30s clip to the whole list ("shot last weekend at {event}"). This is
  the long-game loop — agencies book from the *third* touch, not the first.
- **After any reply → move to WhatsApp/call fast.** Portuguese event business
  closes on the phone, not by email.

**The live mini-demo call is the secret weapon.** A 15-minute video call where you
sing one verse and the canvas visibly draws itself is unforgettable and takes zero
travel. Offer it in every message.

## Seeding the network (do once)

- **One showcase performance, strategically free**: agency open-house, venue
  showcase night, or an industry mixer where 20 planners are in the room. Free
  only for *distributors* (people who resell you), never for end clients.
- **Venues as allies**: the 3–5 Lisbon venues with good projection walls host
  corporate events weekly. Get on their preferred-supplier list; leave them the
  agency sheet. They recommend acts when clients ask.
- **Vendor co-marketing**: fal.ai / ElevenLabs / Ollama case-study posts reach
  exactly the tech-event organizers in Tier B — and cost nothing.

## Cadence & tracking

- **Weekly rhythm**: 10 new contacts researched + first-touched, all due
  follow-ups sent, every reply answered same day. That's ~2 focused hours/week.
- **Tracking**: one spreadsheet/Notion board — contact, segment, last touch, next
  touch date, stage (contacted → replied → call → proposal → booked).
- **Expected math**: cold outreach to a well-picked list converts ~1 booking per
  25–40 contacts in the first quarter; each delivered show typically produces 1–2
  referral inquiries. The flywheel is footage → outreach → show → better footage.

## What NOT to do

- Don't send agencies the tech story (Elixir, Gemma, latency budgets) — send the
  audience story. Keep the tech for conference-organizer pitches only.
- Don't negotiate retail price with an agency's end client — ever. Protect the
  agency's margin and they'll bring you the next event.
- Don't mass-blast a generic email to 500 addresses. 50 researched contacts with
  a named person outperform it and don't burn the market.
