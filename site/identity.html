Sinestesia — identity

Identity · v2

Two senses, one overlap.

Synesthesia is not sound or colour — it is the place where they cross. So the mark is two discs, one the colour of a voice, one the colour of pigment, and the only bright part of it is where they overlap. Nothing is drawn; the overlap is produced by the two shapes meeting, the same way the paintings are.

The two i's

The name holds two i's, evenly spaced near each end. Setting both in ochre puts two points of colour inside the word — the crossing happens in the reading, not in an illustration. Works at any size; in one-colour print they simply go solid.

Overlap is 39%

Discs sit 61% of a diameter apart, so they share 39% of their width — close enough to be one object, far enough that the lens is a shape you notice. Never separate them, never let them fully eclipse. The mark's clear space is one disc radius on all sides.

It moves once

On the site the ochre disc drifts a few percent over seven seconds, so the overlap breathes. That is the accumulate principle at logo scale: the mark is never re-drawn, only re-crossed. Off entirely under reduced motion.
