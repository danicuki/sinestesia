One canvas,
not a slideshow.

Most visuals cut. Yours doesn't. The second verse sits next to the first. The bridge darkens the sky the chorus put there. By the last chorus the wall holds the whole song at once — and the audience watched it arrive.

It paints from what it hears, so it will surprise you. That is the point. Two performances of the same song never give you the same picture.

Questions singers ask

What do you need from us on the night?

A stereo feed off the desk, a projector or LED wall, and one power drop. We bring the machine and an operator. Soundcheck is twenty minutes.

Can I choose the style?

We set a palette and a mood with you beforehand — grain, colour range, how loose the strokes are. The composition itself comes from the song as you sing it.

What if it paints something strange?

Sometimes it will. It is listening, not illustrating. The strange ones are usually the ones the room photographs.

Does it work with a full band, or DJ sets?

Yes. With no lyrics it works from the music alone — slower, more abstract, still one canvas.

Who owns the painting?

You do. The keepsake the audience takes is a print, not the original.

Do I have to explain any crypto on stage?

No. The audience scans a code and gets a picture. That is the whole interaction.

Send us one song and the date.

We come back with a painting from that recording, so you can see it before you commit to it on stage.
