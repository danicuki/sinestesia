What production asks first

Does it take control away from our LD?

No. It owns one output — the screen behind the act. House lights, haze and everything else stay on your desk. We can blackout on your cue at any point.

What happens if the internet drops mid-song?

The painting keeps rendering locally. Only the keepsake step needs a connection, and that can complete after the set.

Do you need the setlist in advance?

Helpful, not required. We ask for reference tracks so the palette is agreed before the show.

Is there anything the audience has to install?

No app. A camera and a browser. The print is free to them.

Can we brand it for an activation?

Yes — palette, aspect ratio and the end card can follow a brand guide. The composition still comes from the performance.

What are you not good at?

Fast cue-heavy sets with a song every two minutes, and stages with no dedicated screen. Both make the painting hard to read.

Tell us the room and the date.

We send the full rider, a fee range for your capacity, and a painting made from a recording of the act you have booked.
